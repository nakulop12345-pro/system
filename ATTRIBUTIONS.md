# Attributions

## External assets used (Earth textures)
Four of the five Earth maps in `assets/textures/` (day, 2k day, night, specular) are derived from **Natural Earth**; the cloud map is procedural (see below). Natural Earth states that all its raster and vector data are in the public domain, no permission is needed and credit is unnecessary (terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ ; also https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/LICENSE.md). They are credited here anyway. Not NASA satellite photography: they are processed cartographic data, regraded and shaded for this app. Natural Earth is not affiliated with this project and does not endorse it.

Source data (all Natural Earth, creators Tom Patterson / US National Park Service for the raster; Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso and contributors for the vector layers):
- Natural Earth II with Shaded Relief and Water, 1:10m raster (`NE2_LR_LC_SR_W.tif`) - https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-raster/tree/master/10m_rasters/NE2_LR_LC_SR_W
- `ne_10m_land` and `ne_10m_lakes` - https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson
- `ne_10m_populated_places_simple` - same folder

## Approximations
Planets and Pluto: J2000 Keplerian elements advanced by mean motion, no secular rates. Moons: circular orbits, arbitrary phases. Comets: approximate elements, heuristic particle tails. Asteroid and Kuiper belts: seeded visual distributions (Kirkwood gaps and a Pluto-resonance clump are included). Eclipse view: educational alignment only. Distances and light times use the simplified model.

## ISS tracking
- **satellite.js 5.0.0** (SGP4/SDP4 propagation), MIT licence, loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net - https://github.com/shashwatak/satellite-js
- **Orbital elements:** fetched in the visitor's browser from CelesTrak's public GP endpoint (https://celestrak.org/NORAD/elements/gp.php?CATNR=25544&FORMAT=TLE) when reachable; no data is bundled from it. Underlying element sets originate from US Space Force / 18th Space Defense Squadron via Space-Track/CelesTrak.
- **Stored fallback TLE:** a public ISS TLE dated 2008-09-20 kept in `js/satellite-data.js`; it is used only for simulation dates within 30 days of its epoch.
- **ISS 3D model:** procedural (no external asset). The ISS ground-track map reuses `assets/textures/earth_day_2k.jpg` (Natural Earth derived, see above).
- Positions are predictions from TLEs (or a clearly labelled simplified circular orbit), not live telemetry.
